## About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Fred Hutch and a Damon Runyon Quantitative Biology Fellow. My research explores the connections between microbial metabolism, host physiology, and infectious disease.

I combine experimental microbiology, biochemical characterization, and metabolomics to investigate how microbes respond to their environments and how these responses influence interactions with the host. I am particularly interested in how hyperoxia and lung injury alter the metabolic environment and shape susceptibility to respiratory infection.

## Research interests

- Microbial metabolism and adaptation to host environments
- Host–pathogen interactions and respiratory infection
- Hyperoxia, lung injury, and metabolic remodeling
- Quantitative approaches to understanding biological mechanisms
